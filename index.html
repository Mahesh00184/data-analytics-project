Critical Finding: Discount Threshold Vulnerability

Data modeling revealed that promotional discount rates exceeding 18% to 20% produce negative margin yields across the Furniture category (especially Tables and Bookcases) due to high base logistics and wholesale overheads.

Strategic Recommendation: Terminate flat percentage discounting. Transition towards threshold-based tiered vouchers (e.g. ₹500 off on cart value > ₹5,000) or bundle accessories.

Regional Hubs & Category Stability

The South Region generates ~38% of company-wide sales with top-performing clusters centered in Chennai, Coimbatore, and Bangalore.

Cross-Selling Opportunity: Office Supplies boasts an exceptional and consistent gross margin (>22%). Use high-traffic tech sales to cross-sell office essentials for immediate gross margin expansion.
